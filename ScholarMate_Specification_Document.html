1. Executive Project Overview

ScholarMate is an end-to-end academic exam acceleration platform engineered specifically for engineering diploma and undergraduate students. Developed to bridge the severe gap between generic rote-memorization and syllabus-grounded mastery, ScholarMate ingests institutional curricula, textbook chapters, and past semester university papers to generate active recall flashcards, timed mock exam simulations, intelligent process study timers, and AI-predicted 50+ question banks with marking scheme evaluation.

1.1 Problem Statement & Academic Value Proposition

The Academic Bottleneck: Engineering and polytechnic students face high failure rates in board examinations (such as Operating Systems, Data Structures, and Computer Networks) due to:
  • Inability to prioritize high-weightage derivations (10-mark questions) over minor definitions.
  • Lack of timed answer practice that adheres to strict board marking schemes.
  • Generic AI chatbots producing unstructured, conversational essays without marking-scheme rubrics.
ScholarMate Solution: Delivers deterministic question frequency heatmaps, interactive 1-click time limit converters, voice-enabled viva voce drills, and answer evaluation scoring rubrics mapped directly to university marks.

4. Core Applications & Functional Modules

4.1 Nexa AI Tutor & Multi-Mode Socratic Coach

Unlike conventional chatbots that generate sprawling generic text, Nexa AI is engineered with 5 structured academic modes:

  • 📖 Intuitive Concept Explanations: Breaks complex engineering theorems into real-world analogies (e.g., explaining Semaphore locks via ATM access lines).
  • ⚡ 60-Second Revision Cheat-Sheets: Bulleted axioms, laws, and critical formula lists designed for rapid 5-minute pre-exam cramming.
  • 📝 Exam Question & Marking Breakdown: Generates 3-mark definitions, 7-mark diagrams, and 10-mark derivations paired with step-by-step model answers and point allocations.
  • 🔢 Step-by-Step Problem Solver: Multi-step mathematical proof traces with intermediate steps for numerical subjects.
  • ⚠️ Examiner Trap Analysis: Highlights common mistakes that lead to lost marks on board exams.

4.2 5-Year Paper Predictor Engine (50+ Questions Architecture)

Deterministic Question Frequency Analysis: Analyzes historical patterns across 5 years of university semester examination papers to predict questions with high statistical probability:
  • 20 High-Priority Compulsory Pool (90–100% Probability): 10-mark derivations, algorithm proofs, and architectural blueprints that recur cyclically every 1–2 semesters.
  • 30 Less-Priority Supplementary Pool (60–80% Probability): 3-mark short notes, comparative differences, and technical definitions required to ensure 100% paper coverage.
  • Unit Frequency Heatmap: Visual breakdown of syllabus modules (Unit 1 to Unit 5) showing historical mark weightage percentages and risk indicators.

4.3 Smart Process Focus Timer & Time Limit Conversion Engine

Converts ambiguous study tasks into scientifically grounded time limits with 1-click presets:

Study Process Recommended Time Cognitive Rationale & Board Exam Application
10-Mark Core Derivation / Numerical 45 Mins High-yield analytical flow state for Banker's Algorithm, Page Replacement, QuickSort, and Subnetting derivations.
7-Mark Architecture & Diagrams 25 Mins Standard Pomodoro block for drawing architecture schemes, block diagrams, and comparative matrices.
3-Mark Definitions & Formula Sprint 15 Mins High-intensity active recall drill for memorizing definitions, axioms, laws, and equations.

4.4 Answer Arena with Automated Syllabus Evaluation

Allows students to type answers under simulated examination constraints. The answer is parsed by the AI evaluation microservice against a 4-point board grading rubric:

  • Technical Accuracy & Definition Rigor (40% Weightage)
  • Step-by-Step Derivation Completeness (30% Weightage)
  • Key Terminology & Nomenclature Usage (20% Weightage)
  • Examiner Impression & Presentation (10% Weightage)

5. Backend Architecture & Database Schema

5.1 Multi-Model AI Orchestration Pipeline (src/lib/gemini.ts)

ScholarMate features a multi-tiered failover orchestration architecture that guarantees 100% uptime even under upstream API outages or quota limits:

  1. Primary Tier — Google Gemini Engine: Calls gemini-2.5-flash and gemini-2.0-flash with direct REST endpoints and fallback to official @google/genai SDK.
  2. Secondary Tier — OpenAI GPT-4o Mini: Activated if an OpenAI key is configured.
  3. Tertiary Tier — OpenRouter DeepSeek V3: High-reasoning fallback for complex mathematical proofs.
  4. Quaternary Tier — HuggingFace Mistral-7B: Low-latency open-weights fallback.
  5. Deterministic Heuristic Knowledge Engine: Offline rule-based syllabus engine guaranteeing instant responses even without network access.

5.2 Relational Database Schema (Prisma ORM & Neon PostgreSQL)
